# 615 Vinyl Laymen's Terms Framework

## Scope
615 Vinyl is a custom vinyl design and production business that turns customer ideas into product-ready designs, pricing, scheduling, inventory decisions, and fulfillment communication.

## Rough Pass
- I: a handmade boutique brand with a storefront, custom studio, and production workflow
- O: created from a personal craft business built around custom vinyl and small-batch creativity
- K: website, customer orders, inventory, design proofs, pricing, scheduling, shipping, and supplier data
- F: collect orders, confirm design intent, track production, manage supply needs, and deliver finished goods
- M: customer trust, owner judgment, workflow awareness, and communication quality
- R: connects customer, design, production, supplier, and fulfillment systems
- T: grows from a small local storefront into a scalable custom fulfillment business

## Deepened 7 Primes
### I – Identity
- Who is it? A boutique vinyl business, built around personal creativity and practical craft work.
- What makes it different? The brand blends handmade design, custom request handling, and home-based production.
- [SOC] The storefront is the public face; the production system is the internal engine.
- [PSY] Customers want to feel seen, not just sold to.

### O – Origin
- Created from a personal creative practice and a need to sell custom design work.
- Roots are in small-batch handmade work, local market presence, and custom-request communication.
- Most value came from direct trust, word-of-mouth, and repeat buyers.

### K – Composition
- Frontend storefront and product catalog
- Custom design request intake
- Pricing engine with redesign and rush-fee logic
- Inventory tracking for supplies and materials
- Production queue and timeline logic
- Shipping and fulfillment tracking
- Customer communication and approval flow

### F – Function
- Turn customer ideas into a producible design
- Price projects based on labor, materials, and complexity
- Track what inventory is needed and when
- Estimate completion dates and shipping ETA
- Keep customer communication clear and simple

### M – Mind
- The business has a human decision layer: Christine’s judgment, pricing, design approval, and production capacity.
- The system should help the owner think clearly, not replace her discernment.
- Observer relevance: customers need confidence that the process is professional, understandable, and transparent.

### R – Relation
- Customer to business
- Business to supplier
- Design to production
- Production to shipping
- Sales to supply planning
- Customer to timeline expectations

### T – Trajectory
- Today: static storefront + custom order flow
- Next: login, inventory, supplier price comparison, and scheduling system
- Future: integrated dashboard for reordering, queue limits, shipping tracking, and business forecasting

## Consistency Check
- K supports F: yes, because the inventory and scheduling systems support production and customer expectations.
- O explains I: yes, the business identity comes from a personal handmade craft origin.
- M makes sense with K and R: yes, because the system is designed to support human decisions and communication.
- T harmonizes with O + F: yes, if the business grows without losing handmade quality and customer trust.
